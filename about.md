### Research interests

I love understanding life at the molecular level. I believe that the richest understanding of biology comes from painting a detailed picture of the protein conformational landscape across space and time. I also find that biology is the most rewarding when atomic-level phenomena can be directly tied to functional outcomes for an organism.

I'm excited to be a part of the burgeoning, dynamic field of *de novo* protein design. I think protein design is a fascinating case study in the powers and limitations of generative AI; I believe that protein design will be immensely helpful in developing the next generation of medicines, but only when its use is grounded in a deep understanding of protein science.

### Software and electronics

I'm passionate about open-source software development. I am a founding "1/astronaut" for [Reciprocal Space Station](https://rs-station.github.io/), a group based out of the [Hekstra Lab](https://hekstralab.fas.harvard.edu/) developing open-source crystallographic software. I strongly believe in the importance of engagement and communication between developers and users on platforms such as GitHub.

Outside of my professional work, I am excited about web development, computer architechture, open-source operating systems, and DIY microcontroller electronics. I love engaging with the intersection of hardware and software through projects such as refurbishing an old laptop or designing a custom minature gaming console. When developing this website, I challenged myself to rewrite all of the HTML and CSS from scratch to unsure that I understood how code translated to a live webpage. 

I am the co-founder of [Puntalytics](https://twitter.com/ThePuntRunts), a team developing custom analytics for evaluating NFL punting. Our work has been featured in ESPN, the New York Times, the Athletic, the Ringer, and SB Nation. I developed an open-source R package, [`puntr`](https://puntalytics.github.io/puntr/), to ensure the reproducibility of our analysis and to make it easy for anyone to get started with puntalytics. I have had the pleasure of meeting three NFL punters for interviews, which you can find on the [Puntalytics website](https://puntalytics.github.io/punterviews).
